---
title: On the Psychology of GPT-4
summary: A psychometric study of the personality signals perceived in GPT-4 responses.
date: 2024-01-01
category: Publication
---

This study subjects GPT-4 to a collection of psychometric tests and analyzes the resulting profiles. The work does not claim that a language model possesses a human personality. Instead, it examines the personality-like signals perceived by a person interacting with the system and asks how stable those signals are across repeated tests and parameter settings.

The reported results include higher honesty and humility, lower Machiavellianism and narcissism, moderate anxiety, and above-average performance on verbal cognitive-reflection tasks relative to the human reference groups used by the tests.

**Authors:** Adrita Barua, Gary Brase, Ke Dong, Pascal Hitzler, and Eugene Vasserman
