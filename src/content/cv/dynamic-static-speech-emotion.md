---
title: Dynamic-static Cross Attentional Feature Fusion for Speech Emotion Recognition
summary: Combining static and temporal acoustic features through cross attention.
date: 2023-01-01
category: Publication
---

Speech emotion recognition depends on both static acoustic characteristics and temporal change. Simple concatenation or addition can lose information when these feature families are combined. This work introduces a cross-attentional feature fusion mechanism that joins CNN-derived static features with LSTM-derived dynamic features.

On IEMOCAP, the proposed SD-CAFF model reported weighted and unweighted accuracies of 75.78% and 74.89%. Cross-corpus experiments on MSP-IMPROV were used to examine generalization beyond the training corpus.

**Authors:** Ke Dong, Hao Peng, and Jie Che
