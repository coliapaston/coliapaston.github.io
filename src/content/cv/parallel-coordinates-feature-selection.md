---
title: Parallel Coordinates with Unsupervised Feature Selection
summary: Improving high-dimensional visualization by ranking attributes before plotting.
date: 2021-06-01
category: Publication
---

Parallel coordinates plots can become difficult to read when irrelevant or redundant dimensions are displayed with equal prominence. This work combines parallel coordinates with Laplacian Score, an unsupervised feature-selection method that ranks attributes according to their locality-preserving power.

The resulting interface allows analysts to reduce visual clutter and focus the plot on dimensions that preserve important structure in the data.

**Authors:** Jiaqi Lou, Ke Dong, and Maosen Wang  
**Venue:** IWCMC 2021
