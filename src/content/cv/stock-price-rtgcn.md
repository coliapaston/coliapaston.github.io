---
title: Stock Price Movement Prediction with Relation-guided GCNs
summary: Modeling real-time company interactions and evolving financial relations for stock prediction.
date: 2023-08-25
category: Publication
---

Stock price movement prediction is difficult because market behavior is volatile and companies influence one another through multiple relation types. This work combines an external-attention module with a relation type guided graph convolutional network.

The external-attention component shares company memory across price and sentiment signals. The graph model updates entity nodes and relation edges iteratively, allowing relation representations to evolve during encoding instead of remaining fixed.

**Authors:** Hao Peng, Ke Dong, and Jie Yang  
**Venue:** Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, 126, 106948
