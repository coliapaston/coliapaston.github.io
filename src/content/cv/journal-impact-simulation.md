---
title: Simulating Journal Impact Factor and Article Quality
summary: An agent-based model of authors, articles, journals, and citation behavior.
date: 2021-06-01
category: Publication
---

Journal Impact Factor is widely used in journal evaluation, but its relationship to article quality is difficult to study directly. This work represents the publication system as an agent-based model composed of authors, articles, and journals.

The simulation treats social and bibliometric factors as model parameters, providing a controlled way to examine how journal-level indicators emerge from article-level behavior.

**Authors:** Ke Dong, Yiran Wang, and Wen Fan  
**Venue:** IWCMC 2021
